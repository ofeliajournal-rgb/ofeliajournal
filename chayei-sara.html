Parashat Chayei Sara

פרשת חיי שרה

Shabbat, November 7, 2026 · Genesis 23:1–25:18 · 105 verses

← All portions

Sarah dies at one hundred and twenty-seven, and Abraham buys a field with a cave — the cave of Machpelah, near Hebron — to bury her, the first piece of the promised land that Abraham owns. Then Abraham sends his servant to find a wife for Isaac, and at the well Rebekah offers water freely — the kindness that changes everything.
